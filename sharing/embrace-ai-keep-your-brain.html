01 — Embrace

Opting out isn't on the table.

AI isn't a productivity tweak bolted onto the way we already work. It's moving the ground floor — the systems, the workflows, what a "job" even means. So the question was never if.

It was always how.

杨涛: 围绕 AI 去重构业务 — rebuild the work around AI, don't just sprinkle it on top.

02 — Embrace

Tool, or battlefield?

For most of us, AI is a tool. Which makes the job easy to say and hard to do: use it really well.

You've got a hammer — go find every nail.
Then earn a bigger hammer. Then a whole toolbox.

(If AI is a battlefield for you — algorithms, compute, data — that's a different talk. Most of us are here to wield it, not build it.)

03 — Our role

Right where you're clueless.
Wrong where you're sharp.

Notice the pattern? In a field you don't know, AI sounds flawless. In the one you know cold, it's subtly — sometimes loudly — wrong.

The G in AGI is General. It hands you the greatest common denominator of what's known. Not the edge.

The edge is still us. That's the job.

05 — Our role

A2R — AI to Real.

AI gets you a draft in minutes. What it doesn't do is cross the friction of the real world: the edge cases, the supply chain, the taste calls, the last mile.

That friction is the job.
Getting it all the way to real — that's what we're for.

06 — Our role

Know why it works.

You can ship a site in two days now. Great. You're still competing with people who were sharp before any of this — and "the AI made it" is not a moat.

AI is an amplifier. Make sure it's amplifying order — not your mess.

Aim to honestly say: I know the basic principle here. 基本原理 — knowing the why, not just the output.

07 — What to watch

Cognitive debt.

Every time you let AI think for you instead of with you, you swipe a credit card. Feels free today. The bill is your own thinking.

And it's silent. No statement ever arrives — you just slowly notice the ideas come harder.

09 — What to watch

Breathe — this isn't anti-AI.

The lead researcher told reporters not to say "brain rot" or "terrifying." Her actual line:

“LLMs are a tool. They're here to stay. What we actually love are humans.”

— Dr. Nataliya Kos'myna, MIT Media Lab

The question isn't whether to use AI. It's how to use it without losing yourself in the process.

10 — How

So — how. Three habits first.

01 · think

Think first, then ask.

Five minutes of your own outline before the prompt. That's saving, not borrowing.

02 · build

Modify, don't select.

Don't accept the AI's frame. Change it, argue with it, rebuild it. Editing > picking.

03 · load

One AI-free rep a week.

Do a whole task by hand sometimes. The muscle regrows — but only under load.
