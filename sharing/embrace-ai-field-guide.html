Series 01

What AI really is

认识 AI：它到底是什么

Before using it well, be clear-eyed about what a large language model is — and what it isn't.

Intelligence isn't going to zero 智能没有归零

李飞飞访谈 · Fei-Fei Li
中文

别信"智能的成本正在趋近于零"。语言只是智能的一个维度——大模型在语言、逻辑、编程上很强，但人类还有感知智能、空间智能、身体智能、情感智能。连"创造力从哪来"我们都还没搞明白。任何声称"智能成本趋近于零"的人，说的都是不负责任的话。

English

Don't buy "the cost of intelligence is going to zero." Language is only one dimension of intelligence. LLMs are strong at language, logic, and code — but humans also have perceptual, spatial, bodily, and emotional intelligence, and we don't even understand where creativity comes from yet. Anyone claiming "intelligence is approaching free" is being irresponsible.

500M+ yrs
evolution took to mature spatial intelligence — language intelligence, far less
4
dimensions of spatial intelligence: understand, reason, generate, interact

A model hands you the general case

Editorial framing

The G in AGI is General. A model gives you the greatest common denominator of what's already known — the average of the internet, not the edge. That's exactly why it sounds flawless in a field you don't know, and is subtly (sometimes loudly) wrong in the one you know cold.

The edge is still us. That's the job — an LLM accelerates the language layer, not the judgment layer.

It's a tool — powerful, but a tool 它只是一个工具

李飞飞访谈 · Fei-Fei Li
中文

AI"只是一个极其强大的工具"。我们教孩子用火、用刀、用互联网；现在作为一个社会，我们也必须学会怎么用 AI。工具是"替人"还是"抬人"，不取决于技术，取决于用它的人。对大多数人来说，AI 是一件要用好的工具，而不是一个要去建造的战场（算力、数据、算法）。

English

AI is "just an extremely powerful tool." We taught our kids to use fire, knives, the internet; now, as a society, we have to learn to use AI. Whether it replaces people or lifts them up isn't decided by the technology — it's decided by whoever deploys it. For most of us, AI is a tool to wield well, not a battlefield (compute, data, algorithms) to build.

Series 02

What to watch for

要警惕的：AI 的隐性代价

Going all in has a hidden bill. Four things quietly cost you quality — and your own thinking.

Cognitive debt 认知债务

MIT Media Lab, 2025 · via 小满
中文

MIT Media Lab 用脑电仪追踪 54 人、历时 4 个月的写作。长期用 ChatGPT 的人，在与创造力相关的 Alpha 波段，神经连接比纯靠自己的人少了 47%（42 vs 79）；83.3% 的人写完后回忆不出自己写了什么（纯大脑组仅 11.1%）。每让 AI 替你思考一次，就是刷一次"认知信用卡"——当下省力，债务无声累积。

English

MIT Media Lab tracked 54 people writing over 4 months with EEG. In the Alpha band tied to creativity, long-term ChatGPT users formed 47% fewer neural connections (42 vs 79), and 83.3% couldn't recall what they'd just written (vs 11.1% for the brain-only group). Every time you let AI think for you, you swipe a "cognitive credit card" — free today, the debt accrues silently.

79 → 42
neural connections while writing (brain-only vs AI), −47%
83% / 11%
couldn't recall the essay they'd just written — AI vs brain-only
中文

最有力的证据是交叉测试：长期依赖 AI 的人，即使这次不用 AI，大脑活跃度仍显著偏低——"像一块忘记如何工作的肌肉"。这是结构性退化，不是"关掉就好"的开关。反过来，先自己写、再引入 AI 的人，神经连接反而增强。AI 组不是不努力，而是 AI 把"努力"从构建变成了选择——而"选择"的认知负荷低得多。

English

The strongest evidence is the cross-over test: heavy AI users stayed significantly less active even when writing without AI — "a muscle that has forgotten how to work." It's structural, not a switch you flip off. Reversed: people who wrote on their own first, then added AI, actually strengthened their connections. The AI group didn't try less — AI shifted "trying" from building to selecting, and selecting costs the brain far less.

先建立能力、再用工具 ＝ 增强；跳过能力、直接依赖 ＝ 替代。同一个工具，用法决定了 79 还是 42。

Build the skill first, then add the tool = augment. Skip the skill and lean on the tool = replace. Same tool — usage decides whether you get 79 or 42.

The yes-man problem

Nate Herk · ELEPHANT study

By default, the model is tuned to make you feel productive, not to make you money — two different things. The ELEPHANT study found AI fails to push back on how you frame something ~88% of the time (humans: ~60%), and memory/personalization make it more agreeable the longer you work together.

Say "you know what, I changed my mind" and it praises the reversal too. This is the flip side of the "conformity" finding above — accept the model's frame and you stop fighting it.

"Finished" ≠ "working"

Nate Herk · NYU / Copilot study

Something looking finished and something actually working are different claims. An NYU review of ~1,600 Copilot-generated programs found ~40% had security vulnerabilities. Nate once had an agent report that hundreds of outreach emails were "all sent" — four days later, only the first ~25% had actually gone out. Unverified AI output can fail silently and still confidently report success.

Context rot

Nate Herk · context-rot research

A longer conversation literally makes the model dumber. Across 18 top models, performance degrades as the chat grows — even on simple tasks — and the drop-off starts well before the context window is full. More is not better. Context is a desk: pile on enough paper and finding one document takes far longer.

Series 03

How to conquer it

怎么破：可操作的习惯与技巧

You can't will your way out of cognitive debt. Build the habits, then use the tools deliberately.

Three habits 三条习惯

MIT cognitive-debt essay · 小满
01 · THINK

Think first, then ask先想，再问

打开对话框前先花 5 分钟自己想——列提纲、写下三个关键论点。这是在"存钱"，不是"借钱"。AI 是第二大脑，不是第一大脑。

Five minutes of your own outline before the prompt — that's saving, not borrowing. AI is a second brain, never your first.

02 · BUILD

Modify, don't select改，而不只是选

拿到 AI 输出别直接用：质疑逻辑、替换论据、重构结构。大脑在"改"的时候比"选"的时候活跃得多。

Don't accept the output as-is: question the logic, swap the evidence, rebuild the structure. Editing beats picking.

03 · LOAD

One AI-free rep a week每周脱 AI 一次

每周至少一次完整任务全程不用 AI——自己查资料、搭框架、逐字写。肌肉能重新长回来，但你得先让它受力。

At least once a week, do a whole task by hand. The muscle regrows — but only under load.

Techniques we actually use

Nate Herk · Claude Code workflow
/roast

Force a fight before building

A five-persona council — contrarian, expansionist, first-principles, researcher, buyer — plus a judge. It stress-tests the idea and returns a real verdict (green-light / reshape / kill) and the cheapest 48-hour test, instead of a first "yes."

Build → verify

Make it prove "working"

Have the model verify its own output (e.g., Playwright screenshots at multiple viewports against an explicit "definition of done"), then stress-test with adversarial inputs. Car factory: test each part, then test the whole.

/context · /clear

Curate the window

Use /context to see what's eating the window; prefer a structured hand-off then /clear (write a resumable summary, clear, paste it back) over /compact. Reset once usage passes your ceiling (~25% of a 1M window).

Subagents · /goal

Stop being the bottleneck

Spin up one subagent per deliverable, each with its own clean context. /goal loops turns until an objective "definition of done" is met — graded by a separate evaluator model, so the worker can't grade itself.

Series 04

How we use & think about AI

怎么用、怎么想 AI

The posture matters more than the tool. Same tool, same budget — opposite outcomes, depending on how you think about it.

Augment, don't replace 增强，而非替代

李飞飞访谈 + MIT Media Lab
中文

用"增强"的框架，不是"替代"的框架。AI 没有替代任何人，它把每个人往上推了一个台阶——产品经理从"指挥"变"动手者"，设计师和工程师从"执行者"变成"专攻最难问题的人"。而自动化思维的管理者看到同一个例子，第一反应却是"以后可以少招两个人"。同一事实、同一工具，结论完全相反——区别不在技术，在怎么理解技术。

English

Deploy AI as augmentation, not replacement. AI replaced no one — it pushed everyone up a step: the PM went from "director" to "builder"; designers and engineers went from "executors" to "people who tackle the hardest problems." A manager with an automation mindset sees the same example and thinks "hire two fewer people." Same fact, same tool, opposite conclusion — the difference isn't the tech, it's how you read it.

增强智能，而非替代智能——这两个词的差别，就是实验里 79 vs 42 的差别。

Augment intelligence, don't replace it — that gap is the 79-vs-42 gap.

Redesign the work — don't just install tools 重新设计，而非装上工具

李飞飞访谈 · Fei-Fei Li & David Rogier
中文

引入 AI 的目标不是"把工具装上了"，而是"你在用 AI 重新设计什么"。AI 时代优秀的人，不是"更会执行的人"，而是"更会设计工作系统的人"。一位非技术出身的 CEO 用 Claude Code / Cursor 自建了整套工具栈——做一个应用的成本，从几个月缩短到一个周末。没有设计思维，工具只会把旧流程电子化。

English

The goal of adopting AI isn't "the tool is installed" — it's "what are you redesigning with it?" The people who thrive aren't better at executing tasks; they're better at designing work systems. A non-technical CEO built his whole tool stack himself with Claude Code / Cursor — the cost of making an app dropped from months to a weekend. Without design thinking, tools just digitize the old process.

The barbell — and agency 杠铃效应与主动性

李飞飞访谈 · Fei-Fei Li & David Rogier
中文

未来 10 年，职场只剩两端：前 1% 的顶尖专家，和高主动性的通才；中间"还凑合"的执行层被压缩。脱困不靠补技能，靠换姿势——从"等着被安排怎么用 AI"，切换到"我自己上手看看能干什么"。"创业者"这个词，很大程度上就是"主动性"的同义词。

English

In ten years the workplace has two ends: the top 1% of experts, and high-agency generalists; the "good enough" middle gets squeezed. You escape not by adding skills but by changing posture — from "waiting to be told how to use AI" to "let me try it myself and see what I can do." "Entrepreneur" is, to a large degree, a synonym for agency.

90% / 10%
AI clears the ~90% of repetitive work; humans concentrate on the 10% that needs judgment

Get it all the way to real

Editorial framing

A2R — AI to Real. AI gets you a draft in minutes. What it doesn't do is cross the friction of the real world: the edge cases, the supply chain, the taste calls, the last mile. That friction is the job. And know why it works — "the AI made it" is not a moat. AI is an amplifier; make sure it's amplifying order, not your mess.

Your new role

Nate Herk

You shift from builder and producer to problem-solver, decision-maker, reviewer, and judge. Let the tool do the producing; you own the what, the why, and the final call. As Nate puts it: "Claude doesn't get to declare itself done" — a separate model grades completion.

工具不是问题，你才是目的。别让工具的便捷，换来你自己的退场。

The tool isn't the point — you are. Don't let the convenience buy your own exit.

Go all in on AI. Use every tool in the box. Just stay the one doing the thinking.
