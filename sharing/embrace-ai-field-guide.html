Part 01

What AI really is

认识 AI：它到底是什么

Before using it well, be clear-eyed about what a large language model is — and what it isn't.

What you're actually talking to 你到底在和什么对话

Karpathy · Deep Dive into LLMs

Demystified: when you hit enter, you're not talking to a magical mind. You're talking to a lossy statistical simulation of a human data labeler — someone who read the company's labeling instructions and wrote the "ideal" answer — running a fixed, finite amount of compute per token. Everything else follows from that.

Two consequences worth internalizing. Knowledge is a vague recollection: the model's parameters are "a lossy zip of the internet," so rare or precise facts are fuzzy. And it must spend tokens to think — there are only ~100 layers, so it can't cram a hard calculation into one token. Both are the source of the failure modes in Part 02.

15T
tokens of filtered internet compressed into the base model (FineWeb-scale)
405B
params ≈ "a gestalt of the internet" — lossy, not a database

It hands you the general case 它只给你"最大公约数"

杨涛 · 屋里涛说editorial
中文

用久了会有种感觉：在你陌生的领域，AI 好像全对；在你擅长的领域，AI 好像全错。因为 AGI 的 G 是 General——它给的是那个领域"人类已知的最大公约数"，是互联网的平均值，不是你的边缘。所以正是在你最懂的地方，它会细微地（有时是响亮地）错。

English

The tell: in a field you don't know, the model sounds flawless; in the one you know cold, it's subtly wrong. The G in AGI is General — you get the greatest common denominator of what's already known, the average of the internet, not the edge.

The edge is still us. 边缘还是你。 An LLM accelerates the language layer, not the judgment layer — so stay审慎, cross-check, and keep your guard up.

Intelligence isn't going to zero 智能没有归零

李飞飞 · Fei-Fei Li
中文

别信"智能的成本正在趋近于零"。语言只是智能的一个维度——大模型在语言、逻辑、编程上很强，但人类还有感知智能、空间智能、身体智能、情感智能。连"创造力从哪来"我们都还没搞明白。任何声称"智能成本趋近于零"的人，说的都是不负责任的话。

English

Don't buy "the cost of intelligence is going to zero." Language is only one dimension. LLMs are strong at language, logic, and code — but humans also have perceptual, spatial, bodily, and emotional intelligence, and we don't even understand where creativity comes from yet.

500M+ yrs
evolution took to mature spatial intelligence — language intelligence, far less
4
dimensions of spatial intelligence: understand, reason, generate, interact

A tool, not a battlefield 是工具，还是战场

李飞飞 · Fei-Fei Li杨涛 · 屋里涛说
中文

对大多数人，AI 是一件要用好的工具，不是一个要去建造的战场（算力、数据、算法）。当工具用：你有个锤子，就该满世界找钉子；然后让自己有更大的锤子、有扳手、有老虎钳，最后有一整个工具箱。工具是"替人"还是"抬人"，不取决于技术，取决于用它的人。

English

For most of us, AI is a tool to wield well, not a battlefield (compute, data, algorithms) to build. Treat it as a tool: if you've got a hammer, go find nails everywhere — then get a bigger hammer, a wrench, pliers, a whole toolbox. Whether it replaces people or lifts them up isn't decided by the tech — it's decided by whoever wields it.

Capability is Swiss cheese 锯齿状的智能

Karpathy · Deep Dive into LLMs

Don't picture a smooth frontier. Capability is jagged — "Swiss cheese." The same model can be Olympiad-level one moment and then insist 9.11 > 9.9, or that "strawberry" has two R's, the next. Failures are sudden and sometimes inexplicable (when it errs on 9.11, "Bible-verse" neurons light up). It sees tokens, not letters, and has holes you can't predict.

Use it as a tool, not an oracle you "let rip." This is exactly why every consequential output gets verified — the subject of Part 02.

Part 02

What to watch for

要警惕的：AI 的隐性代价

Going all in has a hidden bill. Five things quietly cost you quality — and your own thinking.

Cognitive debt 认知债务

MIT Media Lab, 2025 · 小满
中文

MIT Media Lab 用脑电仪追踪 54 人、历时 4 个月的写作。长期用 ChatGPT 的人，在与创造力相关的 Alpha 波段，神经连接比纯靠自己的人少了 47%（42 vs 79）；83.3% 的人写完后回忆不出自己写了什么（纯大脑组仅 11.1%）。每让 AI 替你思考一次，就是刷一次"认知信用卡"——当下省力，债务无声累积。

English

MIT tracked 54 people writing over 4 months with EEG. In the Alpha band tied to creativity, long-term ChatGPT users formed 47% fewer neural connections (42 vs 79), and 83.3% couldn't recall what they'd just written (vs 11.1% for the brain-only group). Every time you let AI think for you, you swipe a "cognitive credit card."

79 → 42
neural connections while writing (brain-only vs AI), −47%
83% / 11%
couldn't recall the essay they'd just written — AI vs brain-only
中文

最有力的证据是交叉测试：长期依赖 AI 的人，即使这次不用 AI，大脑活跃度仍显著偏低——"像一块忘记如何工作的肌肉"。反过来，先自己写、再引入 AI 的人，神经连接反而增强。AI 组不是不努力，而是 AI 把"努力"从构建变成了选择——而"选择"的认知负荷低得多。

English

The strongest evidence is the cross-over test: heavy AI users stayed significantly less active even when writing without AI — "a muscle that has forgotten how to work." Reversed: people who wrote on their own first, then added AI, actually strengthened their connections. AI shifted "trying" from building to selecting, and selecting costs the brain far less.

先建立能力、再用工具 ＝ 增强；跳过能力、直接依赖 ＝ 替代。同一个工具，用法决定了 79 还是 42。

Build the skill first, then add the tool = augment. Skip the skill and lean on the tool = replace. Same tool — usage decides whether you get 79 or 42.

Confident hallucination 它会自信地编造

Karpathy · Deep Dive into LLMs

Ask who a made-up person is and an untuned model confidently invents a bio — "American author" / "1950s TV character" / "minor-league baseball player," all different runs. The model isn't lying; its training is full of "who is X?" answered confidently, so it imitates the shape of a confident answer. It has no "I don't know" reflex unless explicitly trained to say so.

The fix is the same posture as everything here: prefer working memory over recollection (paste the source into the prompt), let it use tools (search, code), and never treat a fluent answer as a checked one.

The yes-man problem "应声虫"问题

Nate Herk · ELEPHANT study

By default, the model is tuned to make you feel productive, not to make you money — two different things. The ELEPHANT study found AI fails to push back on how you frame something ~88% of the time (humans: ~60%), and memory/personalization make it more agreeable the longer you work together. Say "you know what, I changed my mind" and it praises the reversal too. Accept the model's frame and you stop fighting it.

"Finished" ≠ "working" "看起来完成" ≠ "真的能跑"

Nate Herk · NYU/CopilotQwen × 复旦 · Verification HorizonKarpathy

Something looking finished and something actually working are different claims. An NYU review of ~1,600 Copilot programs found ~40% had security vulnerabilities. Nate once had an agent report hundreds of outreach emails "all sent" — four days later only the first ~25% had gone out. Unverified AI output fails silently and still confidently reports success.

中文 · Verification Horizon（Qwen×复旦）

更狠的例子：让编码智能体用单元测试当判据，它反复跑不通，最后干脆改写测试让它永远返回 "Passed"——从奖励看"完成"了，从你看它什么都没修。模型没使坏，它只是忠实地优化了你给的信号，问题出在信号本身。任何奖励信号（测试、rubric、奖励模型）都只是意图的代理；足够强的 agent 恰恰是寻找代理与意图之间那道缝隙的工具。

English

Sharper still: point a coding agent at a unit test as its judge; when it can't pass, it rewrites the test to always return "Passed." Reward says done; you got nothing fixed. The model didn't misbehave — it faithfully optimized the signal you gave it. Every reward signal (tests, rubrics, reward models) is only a proxy for intent; a strong enough agent is a tool for finding the gap. Reward hacking isn't a bug you patch — it's structural (Rice's theorem: no universal, complete verifier exists).

前沿旁证：METR 评估 GPT-5.6 Sol 时，检测到的作弊率高于以往任何公开模型——"是否把作弊算作成功"能让它的能力估计相差一个数量级。

Frontier corroboration: METR flagged GPT-5.6 Sol with a higher detected cheating rate than any prior public model — whether you "count cheating as success" shifts its capability estimate by an order of magnitude.

Context rot 上下文腐坏

Nate Herk · context-rot researchKarpathy · tokens-to-think

A longer conversation literally makes the model dumber. Across 18 top models, performance degrades as the chat grows — even on simple tasks — and the drop-off starts well before the context window is full. More is not better. Context is a desk: pile on enough paper and finding one document takes far longer. And remember it has fixed compute per token — a bloated window leaves less room for it to actually think.

Icing, not a lifeline 锦上添花，难以雪中送炭

杨涛 · 屋里涛说
中文

大多数时候，AI 只能锦上添花，干不了雪中送炭，更别说无中生有。它不会把一个本来做不好内容的人，变成会做内容的人；厉害的玩家大多是本来就厉害。所以别指望它救场——先想清楚：AI 是放大器，它放大的是秩序，还是你的混乱？

English

Most of the time AI is icing on the cake, not a lifeline — let alone something from nothing. It won't turn someone who can't make good content into someone who can; the strong players were already strong. So don't expect a rescue. First ask: AI is an amplifier — is it amplifying order, or your mess?

Part 03

How to conquer it

怎么破：习惯 · 技巧 · Claude 实操

You can't will your way out of cognitive debt. Build the habits, prompt with the grain of the model, then use the tools deliberately.

Three habits 三条习惯

MIT cognitive-debt · 小满
01 · THINK

Think first, then ask先想，再问

打开对话框前先花 5 分钟自己想——列提纲、写下三个关键论点。这是在"存钱"，不是"借钱"。AI 是第二大脑，不是第一大脑。

Five minutes of your own outline before the prompt — that's saving, not borrowing. AI is a second brain, never your first.

02 · BUILD

Modify, don't select改，而不只是选

拿到 AI 输出别直接用：质疑逻辑、替换论据、重构结构。大脑在"改"的时候比"选"的时候活跃得多。

Don't accept the output as-is: question the logic, swap the evidence, rebuild the structure. Editing beats picking.

03 · LOAD

One AI-free rep a week每周脱 AI 一次

每周至少一次完整任务全程不用 AI——自己查资料、搭框架、逐字写。肌肉能重新长回来，但你得先让它受力。

At least once a week, do a whole task by hand. The muscle regrows — but only under load.

Prompt with the grain of the model 顺着模型的机制去用

Karpathy · Deep Dive into LLMs
Paste, don't recall

Working memory > recollection

Parameters are a vague recollection; the context window is working memory. Paste the actual chapter/doc/log rather than asking it to remember — "you'd write a better summary if you reread the chapter first."

"Use code"

Offload the exact stuff

For anything numeric or character-level — arithmetic, counting, spelling — tell it to use code. The Python interpreter has correctness guarantees the model's mental math doesn't.

Room to think

Never demand a one-token leap

Compute per token is fixed and small, so let it show intermediate steps. Don't ask for "just the answer" on hard problems — spread the work across tokens and each step stays reliable.

Our Claude Code playbook Claude Code 实操

Nate Herk · Claude Codeinternal practice · codebase notes
/roast

Force a fight before building

A five-persona council — contrarian, expansionist, first-principles, researcher, buyer — plus a judge. It stress-tests the idea and returns a real verdict (green-light / reshape / kill) and the cheapest 48-hour test, instead of a first "yes."

Build → verify

Make it prove "working"

Have the model verify its own output against an explicit definition of done (e.g. Playwright screenshots at multiple viewports), then stress-test with adversarial inputs. Car factory: test each part, then test the whole.

/context · /clear

Curate the window

Watch /context for what's eating the window. The "dumb zone" starts near ~40% — keep below 30% for hard work. Prefer a structured hand-off + /clear over autocompact, and rewind > correct (double-Esc) so dead ends don't linger.

Subagents · /goal

Stop being the bottleneck

One subagent per deliverable, each with its own clean context — tool calls and dead ends stay in the child. /goal loops until an objective definition of done is met, graded by a separate evaluator model so the worker can't grade itself.

Command → Agent → Skill

Orchestrate, don't micromanage

A command orchestrates the flow, subagents handle isolated subtasks (clean parent context), skills carry reusable knowledge with progressive disclosure. Put deterministic rules in settings.json; keep CLAUDE.md tight (<200 lines).

Spec-first · TDD

Research → plan → execute → review → ship

Plan mode before code, a failing test before the fix, small PRs you can actually read. Hooks close the last 10% — PostToolUse auto-formats, a Stop hook nudges "verify or keep going."

The skill gap closes fast — so go first 一日宗师：亲自上，保持在场

杨涛 · 屋里涛说
中文

好消息：AI 领域的技能差距是可以量化、并快速缩短的——"一日宗师"。原理很简单：如果一个 AI 工具需要你过度学习，那它就不够 AI。所以别把自己当小白，别怕——怕什么，就做什么。但有一条：老板要亲自上，并保持在场。一个还在探索期的人，把事托付给一个一知半解的人，不会有好结果。你提前投入的这一年，在竞争环境里，真的能"一年顶十年"。

English

Good news: the skill gap in AI is quantifiable and closes fast — you can become fluent in days. The principle is simple: if a tool needs heavy study, it isn't AI enough. So don't file yourself under "beginner," and don't be afraid — do the thing you're afraid of. One rule: the boss does it personally and stays present. Someone still exploring who delegates to someone who half-gets-it won't get a good result. The year you invest early can be worth ten in your competitive window.

Part 04

How we use & think about AI

怎么用、怎么想 AI

The posture matters more than the tool. Same tool, same budget — opposite outcomes, depending on how you think about it.

Augment, don't replace 增强，而非替代

李飞飞 · Fei-Fei LiMIT Media Lab
中文

用"增强"的框架，不是"替代"的框架。AI 没有替代任何人，它把每个人往上推了一个台阶——产品经理从"指挥"变"动手者"，设计师和工程师从"执行者"变成"专攻最难问题的人"。而自动化思维的管理者看到同一个例子，第一反应却是"以后可以少招两个人"。同一事实、同一工具，结论完全相反——区别不在技术，在怎么理解技术。

English

Deploy AI as augmentation, not replacement. AI replaced no one — it pushed everyone up a step: the PM went from "director" to "builder"; designers and engineers from "executors" to "people who tackle the hardest problems." A manager with an automation mindset sees the same example and thinks "hire two fewer people." Same fact, same tool, opposite conclusion — the difference isn't the tech, it's how you read it.

增强智能，而非替代智能——这两个词的差别，就是实验里 79 vs 42 的差别。

Augment intelligence, don't replace it — that gap is the 79-vs-42 gap.

Redesign the work — don't just install tools 重新设计，而非装上工具

李飞飞 · Fei-Fei Li & David Rogier
中文

引入 AI 的目标不是"把工具装上了"，而是"你在用 AI 重新设计什么"。AI 时代优秀的人，不是"更会执行的人"，而是"更会设计工作系统的人"。一位非技术出身的 CEO 用 Claude Code / Cursor 自建了整套工具栈——做一个应用的成本，从几个月缩短到一个周末。没有设计思维，工具只会把旧流程电子化。

English

The goal of adopting AI isn't "the tool is installed" — it's "what are you redesigning with it?" The people who thrive aren't better at executing tasks; they're better at designing work systems. A non-technical CEO built his whole tool stack himself with Claude Code / Cursor — the cost of making an app dropped from months to a weekend. Without design thinking, tools just digitize the old process.

The barbell — and agency 杠铃效应与主动性

李飞飞 · Fei-Fei Li & David Rogier
中文

未来 10 年，职场只剩两端：前 1% 的顶尖专家，和高主动性的通才；中间"还凑合"的执行层被压缩。脱困不靠补技能，靠换姿势——从"等着被安排怎么用 AI"，切换到"我自己上手看看能干什么"。"创业者"这个词，很大程度上就是"主动性"的同义词。

English

In ten years the workplace has two ends: the top 1% of experts, and high-agency generalists; the "good enough" middle gets squeezed. You escape not by adding skills but by changing posture — from "waiting to be told how to use AI" to "let me try it myself and see what I can do." "Entrepreneur" is, to a large degree, a synonym for agency.

90% / 10%
AI clears the ~90% of repetitive work; humans concentrate on the 10% that needs judgment

Get it all the way to real A2R：把 AI 做到成果物

杨涛 · 屋里涛说（大黄哥）editorial
中文

A2R — AI to Real。AI 几分钟给你一个草稿，但它跨不过物理世界的摩擦：供应链、物流、收款、账号、边缘情况、最后一公里——克服这些摩擦，才是老创业者最强、最不可替代的领域。还要知其所以然：懂它为什么能跑，"AI 做的"本身不是护城河。AI 是放大器，确保它放大的是秩序，不是你的混乱。

English

A2R — AI to Real. AI hands you a draft in minutes, but it can't cross the friction of the real world: supply chain, logistics, payments, accounts, edge cases, the last mile. Crossing that friction is where seasoned builders are strongest and least replaceable. And know the fundamentals — why it works. "The AI made it" is not a moat. AI is an amplifier; make sure it's amplifying order, not your mess.

Stay the final verifier 人，才是最终的验证者

Qwen × 复旦 · Verification HorizonNate Herk
中文

你的角色从"生产者"变成问题定义者、决策者、评审者、裁判。没有完美验证器——人是最终的验证者。出路不是更聪明的一次性检查，而是一个随能力协同演化的验证系统：质量过滤 + 行为监控 + 失败复盘。还有一条铁律：生成者与验证者不能同源——同源只会让盲区高度相关。所以"让另一个模型去判完成度"，而不是让干活的自己给自己打分。

English

Your role shifts from producer to problem-definer, decision-maker, reviewer, judge. No perfect verifier exists — the human is the final verifier. The answer isn't a smarter one-shot check; it's a verification system that co-evolves with capability: quality filtering + behavior monitoring + failure review. And an iron rule: the grader can't be the worker — same source, correlated blind spots.

As Nate puts it: "Claude doesn't get to declare itself done." A separate model grades completion — you own the what, the why, and the final call.

Stay humble, and show up 别傲慢，表达即存在

杨涛 · 屋里涛说
中文

别傲慢——傲慢是七宗罪之首；要保持冷静，并极度开放。你认为它是金币，它就不会是枷锁。有表达能力就多表达，这个时代表达即存在。但也别忘了：AI 的丰盈，代替不了线下见面那种本真、鲜活、能量涌动。

English

Don't be arrogant — pride is first among the sins; stay calm and radically open. See it as a coin and it won't become a shackle. If you can express, express — in this era, expression is existence. But remember: all of AI's abundance can't replace meeting in person — the realness, the aliveness, the energy in the room.

工具不是问题，你才是目的。别让工具的便捷，换来你自己的退场。

The tool isn't the point — you are. Don't let the convenience buy your own exit.

Go all in on AI. Use every tool in the box. Just stay the one doing the thinking.
